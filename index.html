How it works

Two ways to capture a workout

Found a workout somewhere else, or just want to start moving? Either way, Fitoor turns it into something you can track.

Import with AI

Paste text, share a link, or upload a screenshot or PDF — AI extracts the content, parses it into structured data, and matches every exercise into a reusable workout template.

Track with AI

No template needed — log freely in natural language as you train, and AI structures what you did into a session in real time.
